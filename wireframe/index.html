You probably recognize one of these
Tech and biotech employees

Your stock vests faster than you can decide what to do with it. You work in Kendall Square or along 128, and so does most of your net worth.

  • Sell or hold RSUs when they vest
  • When to exercise ISOs, and how much AMT that triggers
  • Whether to buy ESPP shares, and when to sell them
  • Concentrated stock before or after a liquidity event
  • Life coverage sized to a young family
Physicians, residency through attending

You started earning late and the decisions came all at once. Resident at a Boston teaching hospital or attending a few years out.

  • Own-occupation disability sized to your specialty
  • Student loans versus saving for retirement
  • Order to fund 401(k)/403(b), Roth IRA and HSA
  • The income jump after training
Small business owners

Your own plan keeps getting pushed. 5 to 50 people in MetroWest, no plan or one nobody has looked at in years.

  • Standalone 401(k) versus a pooled employer plan
  • Plan design: eligibility, match, safe harbor
  • Small-employer startup tax credits
  • Your own retirement and what happens to the business
  • Buy-sell and key person coverage
