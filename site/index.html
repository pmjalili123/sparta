Why I do this

Most people my age don't have someone in their corner for the financial decisions that matter early on. I try to be that person.

My family came to this country from Iran in the '90s with $1,000. I grew up watching them build everything from scratch, and that shaped how I think about money, risk, and what it means to build something that lasts.

My parents came here for their PhDs. My father studied mechanical engineering at UConn and my mother studied microbiology at Clemson. I come from a family of engineers, scientists and doctors.

I started in aerospace engineering at the University of Alabama. During COVID I got into the markets, and I switched to finance and real estate. Engineers' kids end up in planning for the same reason they end up in engineering: you want to build something that lasts, and you check the math before you trust it.

I live in Newton with Rosie, my Pembroke Welsh Corgi. When I'm not working, I'm usually out for a run.
