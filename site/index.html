About

Why I do this

Most people my age don't have someone in their corner for the financial decisions that matter early on. I try to be that person.

My family came to this country in the '90s with $1,000. I grew up watching them build everything from scratch, and that shaped how I think about money, risk, and what it means to build something that lasts.

I come from a family of engineers, scientists and doctors. I started in aerospace engineering at the University of Alabama, then switched to finance and real estate. Today I'm a financial advisor with Baystate Financial in Southborough.

I live in Newton with my Corgi, Rosie. When I'm not working, I'm usually out for a run.
